               FADE IN:


           1   EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - NIGHT                                    1

               Rain hammers the rocks. A single beam sweeps the black water.

               The door at the base of the tower bangs open. MARA (30s,
               oilskin coat, soaked) staggers out with a lantern.

                                   MARA
                             (shouting)
                         Dad! Dad, the lamp's failing!

               No answer but the wind.


           2   INT. LIGHTHOUSE - LAMP ROOM - CONTINUOUS                   2

               Brass and glass. The great lens turns, slower than it should.
               OWEN (70s) sits on the floor, back to the wall, a wrench in
               his lap.

                                   OWEN
                         It's not the lamp. It's the
                         clockwork. It always was the
                         clockwork.

                                   MARA
                         Then we fix the clockwork.

                      OWEN (CONT'D)                  MARA
               With what? Your mother's       If that's what it takes.
               hairpins?

               The lens stops.

                                                                CUT TO:


               MEMORY - THE SAME ROOM, YEARS EARLIER

                         Turn, little light, turn for the
                         sea,
                         Turn till the morning comes back to
                         me.

                                          THE END
